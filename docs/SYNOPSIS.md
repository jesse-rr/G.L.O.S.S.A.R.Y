# G.L.O.S.S.A.R.Y. - Game Premise

## Synopsis
**The Shadows**, nameless consciousnesses stripped of their identities, awaken in a fractured central Hub. To reclaim their lost meaning, 1 to 3 players must journey cooperatively through distinct, decaying realms—the scorching Desert, the Forgotten Abandoned wastes, and the grinding Mechanic labyrinth. 

Living runes are discovered and mastered, allowing the Shadows to chain them into reality-bending attacks. As language itself begins to break and reality fractures around them, they must conquer the guardians of their respective realms. With each piece of knowledge recovered, their power grows. 

Ultimately, the Shadows must unite at the absolute peak of the world—The Summit—to face a mythical god in a final, conjoined battle. Ascend together and claim meaning, or face the consequences of ignorance and fade back into silence.
